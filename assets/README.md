# NortIT Asset Manifest

This directory documents the original visual assets used by the landing page. Runtime files are stored in `public/assets/` so Next.js can serve them from `/assets/<file>`.

## Art Direction

- Premium editorial technical illustration; never generic stock photography.
- Deep navy and charcoal base with restrained electric-blue light.
- Calm, precise, trustworthy mood with clear silhouettes and crop-safe framing.
- No text, numbers, brands, logos, watermarks, faces, or excessive cyberpunk effects.
- Keep the existing hero photograph unchanged.

## Final Generation Prompts

All seven images were generated with the built-in image model. Every prompt used this shared direction:

> Premium landing-page illustration, landscape 16:10. Sophisticated semi-isometric 3D editorial rendering with physically plausible matte metal, glass, and polymer materials. Deep navy `#060914` and charcoal `#101727`, restrained electric blue `#2f6df6`, pale steel-blue highlights, subtle technical grid, soft ambient haze, controlled rim light, crop-safe composition, clear at card size. Calm, precise, trustworthy, premium. Illustration rather than photography; no people, faces, text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, brands, watermarks, excessive neon, or cyberpunk clutter.

### `support-diagnostics.png`

> Show an open modern laptop and compact desktop with a few carefully separated internal components, a diagnostic probe, subtle signal paths, and one clearly identified fault point. The scene must feel orderly and solvable, never broken or chaotic. Center the hardware slightly right with generous negative space.

### `workstation-setup.png`

> Show a refined monitor, laptop dock, keyboard, mouse, headset, and secure access token arranged as one ready-to-use employee workstation. Subtle blue connection paths communicate configured accounts, email, applications, and access without readable UI. Center the workstation slightly left.

### `network-infrastructure.png`

> Show a compact network cabinet with switch, router, patch panel, backup device, and perfectly organized blue data cables. Restrained luminous paths connect a laptop, Wi-Fi access point, and backup storage as one clean small-business system. Center the cabinet slightly right.

### `home-it-support.png`

> Show a clean home desk with a modern laptop opened for repair, its back panel neatly removed, one SSD and memory module, plus a compact home Wi-Fi router. One subtle blue diagnostic path suggests repair, upgrade, data transfer, and Wi-Fi setup. Keep the scene approachable and uncluttered.

### `clear-scope.png`

> Show four precisely aligned dark-metal modules on a blueprint surface: device, network, secure data, and final check. A thin blue boundary surrounds only the selected modules and one illuminated path joins them in logical order, communicating an exact agreed scope without checklist UI.

### `realistic-schedule.png`

> Show a mechanical timeline made of three solid dark-metal stages that lock together in sequence. A minimal clock mechanism hovers over the active stage and one measured blue pulse moves steadily along the route. The stages feel deliberately spaced and achievable, not rushed.

### `direct-contact.png`

> Show exactly two endpoints: a small client workstation and an open diagnostic workstation with tools. One uninterrupted blue line passes through a central coupling made from two interlocking precision parts, communicating direct responsibility without a call center or intermediaries.
